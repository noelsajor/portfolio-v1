# 🎁 Client Handoff Manual

Welcome to your new Agency Master Template website! This guide is designed to help you manage your content, update your portfolio, and keep your site running smoothly—no coding required.

## ✍️ Updating Your Content
Your website uses a "Content-First" system, meaning you can update your projects and case studies by editing simple text files.

### How to add a new Case Study:
1.  Navigate to the `src/content/case-studies/` folder.
2.  Create a new file ending in `.mdx` (e.g., `my-new-project.mdx`).
3.  Copy the header from an existing file and update the details (Name, Date, Summary).
4.  Write your content in plain text below the header. The site will automatically format it for you!

## 🖼️ Image Best Practices
To keep your site lightning fast for all users, follow these rules for images:
- **Format**: Use `.webp` or `.jpg` whenever possible.
- **Size**: Keep images under 500KB.
- **Dimensions**: If an image is meant to span the whole screen, ensure it is at least 1920px wide.

## 🚀 Managing Your Deployment
Your site is hosted on **Vercel**. 
- **Automatic Updates**: Every time you save a change and "Commit" it to your repository, Vercel will automatically rebuild your site and go live within 60 seconds.
- **Dashboard**: You can view your site analytics and performance scores by logging into the Vercel Dashboard using your provided account.

## 🛡️ Support & Maintenance
- **Backups**: Since your site lives on GitHub, every change you've ever made is backed up automatically.
- **Security**: Security updates are handled automatically by the Next.js framework.
- **Questions?**: If you encounter an issue that isn't covered here, reach out to your account manager.
