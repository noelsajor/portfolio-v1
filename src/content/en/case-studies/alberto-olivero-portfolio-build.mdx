## The real problem

Alberto is a graphic designer and he came with ideas, not with a brief. He asked for help building his site, and what I brought was channelling them: working out what belonged, what did not, and in what shape.

What came out is deliberately simple to navigate. The site's personality lives in the visuals, tied to his personal brand, rather than in the structure, which gets out of the way so the work is what you see.

It carries restrained animations and filters for moving through the projects. It is built in Next.js with the App Router, React, TypeScript and Tailwind, and you feel it in use: it loads and moves fast. The mobile Lighthouse audit scores 100 on performance and 100 on accessibility.

## Technologies

- **Framework**: Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Tools**: pnpm, Git, Vercel
- **Focus areas**: Content architecture, work-first portfolio, navigation performance
