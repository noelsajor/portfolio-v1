## The real problem

Strike arrived with the logo, identity and packaging already settled, but no store. Working from those as the reference I designed the theme, built it, customized it, and have maintained it since.

The catalog spans eight product lines, across cartridges, pre-rolls, flower, gummies, disposable vapes and dabs, each with its own strains and pack sizes. Give every line its own filtering and you have eight implementations that fall out of sync on the first change, so I built one reusable filter and wired every line into it. The product grid with search and pagination sits on top of that.

What takes this store out of the standard is the regulatory side. Every product needs a visible, verifiable, current Certificate of Analysis: the lab document stating what is actually in it. That is not a field you bolt on at the end, it is a system that has to be maintained. Each product carries a linked, downloadable COA in a dedicated grid, so a customer can check the lab results before buying.

The same regulation ended up deciding the platform. Payment processors treat this product category differently, and the ones that will take it do not fit Shopify's checkout flow. So the store is migrating to WordPress with WooCommerce, where payment can actually be solved. That work is underway, with the preliminary design settled.

The migration also brought a new requirement: a wholesale channel. Anyone who registers is held pending approval, and once approved sees wholesale pricing and products that do not exist for other visitors.
