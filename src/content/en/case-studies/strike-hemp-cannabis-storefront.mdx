## The real problem

Strike arrived with a logo and an identity but no live store. The catalog spans eight product lines, across cartridges, pre-rolls, flower, gummies, disposable vapes, and dabs, each with its own strains and pack sizes. Give every line its own filtering and you have eight implementations that fall out of sync on the first change, so I built one reusable filter and wired every line into it.

What takes this store out of the standard is the regulatory side. Every product needs a visible, verifiable, current Certificate of Analysis: the lab document stating what is actually in it. That is not a field you bolt on at the end, it is a system that has to be maintained. Each product carries a linked, downloadable COA, surfaced through a dedicated grid so a customer can check the lab results before buying, with the ongoing work of keeping them current as new product lands.

On that base I built the product grid with search and pagination, with infinite scroll as the fallback.
