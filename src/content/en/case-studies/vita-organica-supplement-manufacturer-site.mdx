## The real problem

In private-label manufacturing the prospect has to convince themselves you can make their product before they talk to anyone. A "contact us" page convinces nobody of that.

So the site has three lead paths, and the one that matters is the mockup generator: the prospect uploads their label art and sees it placed on the manufacturer's real product formats. It turns "can you make mine?" into something they check for themselves. All three paths run through one transactional email pipeline and land in the sales inbox as actionable leads, not in a database nobody reads.

I built it in Astro with hybrid rendering and Sanity as a headless CMS, so marketing can manage content without touching code, and the interactive pieces as isolated React islands instead of shipping JavaScript across the whole site.

The second half of the engagement was the handover. The repository, the hosting, the CMS project, and the transactional email domain all moved to accounts the client owns, with no agency infrastructure left in the loop. That meant migrating the package manager, rewriting internal build notes as operational guides, and running a DNS cutover with no downtime.

## Technologies

- **Framework**: Astro (hybrid rendering), React islands
- **CMS**: Sanity
- **Languages**: TypeScript, HTML, CSS
