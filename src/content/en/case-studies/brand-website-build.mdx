## The real problem

I was hired to improve the company's visuals. Within days it was clear there was nothing to improve: the identity predated the current shape of the business, it was assembled largely from stock elements, and one person was carrying marketing, design, and the website for all seven divisions.

The underlying problem was that those seven divisions had to read as one brand without losing an identity of their own. I solved it with a variation of the master logo that shifts color per division, so the parent reads first and the product line second. The same logic carries into the site.

The symbol is a hexagon, for what it already means in this industry: network nodes and topology, load distribution, structural strength. One of the references I was given was a Lamborghini, for the hexagonal pattern on the wheels, and that thread runs all the way to the primary blue in the palette.

I built the site in Astro with Tailwind, structuring content around each division so the same base data feeds the home page, the division pages, and every product view without duplicating anything by hand. It is bilingual with light and dark mode, switching without a reload, and wired into the CRM the company already used, so an enquiry enters the sales pipeline instead of dying in a form.
